01 — The Challenge

Why RoadSense Exists

Unmaintained road defects are more than minor inconveniences—they are critical public safety hazards that claim lives and cost billions annually.

150K+ Annual Lives Lost

The Silent Safety Crisis

Road accidents claim over 150,000 lives annually in India alone. Unmarked craters, abrupt road deterioration, and unlit monsoon waterlogging account for thousands of preventable tragedies, especially among two-wheeler riders.

₹12,000Cr Vehicle Damage

Economic & Commuter Burden

Cracked rims, blown suspension struts, bent axles, and collision swerves cost commuters and transport operators immense sums each year while compounding urban traffic jams.

< 30 sec Report Time

Bridging the Civic Action Gap

Traditional municipal complaint hotlines are fragmented and slow. RoadSense equips every citizen with a rapid 30-second map pinpointing tool that immediately warns other drivers while documenting defects publicly.

02 — Platform Capabilities

Engineered for Accuracy & Speed

RoadSense combines modern geospatial web standards with community-powered accountability.

Light Ola/Uber Interactive Map

Crisp, light map tiles powered by OpenStreetMap with high-contrast road networks, street names, and landmark visibility.

GPS Pinpoint & Drag Refinement

Locate hazards with 1-click browser GPS positioning or search any address with instant reverse geocoding suggestions.

Safe Route Proximity Scanner

Integrated free OSRM navigation calculating driving directions while automatically scanning for road hazards within 350 meters of your route.

Hazard Density Heatmaps

Toggle safety heatmaps to identify high-density accident blackspots, severe cluster zones, and chronic waterlogging corridors.

Photographic Evidence Lightbox

Upload clear road defect photos with tamper-safe server-side image validation and full-screen lightbox inspection.

Admin Triage & Status Tracking

Municipal administrators inspect submissions, update status through Reported → In Progress → Resolved, and keep communities informed.

03 — End-to-End Workflow

How RoadSense Protects Drivers

A closed-loop civic safety lifecycle from initial detection to verified repair.

01

Spot & Pinpoint

A commuter encounters a road hazard, opens RoadSense, and drops a pin on the high-visibility light map.

02

Document & Upload

Attach an optional road photograph, select the defect type (Pothole, Damaged Road, Waterlogging), and describe the hazard.

03

Live Broadcast

The report goes live instantly across the map, warning thousands of nearby commuters and route navigation users.

04

Civic Resolution

Safety administrators verify repairs and mark the report Resolved, updating public safety metrics.

04 — Innovation Horizon

The Future Scope of RoadSense

RoadSense is designed to scale from a community reporting web portal into an intelligent, autonomous road infrastructure monitor.

Phase 4

Edge AI Dashcam Detection

Utilizing lightweight computer vision models (TensorFlow.js / ONNX) to automatically classify potholes from windshield phone mounts without manual driver distraction.

Phase 5

Smart Municipal API Dispatch

Direct integrations with municipal grievance redressal portals (e.g. CPGRAMS, Swachhata, Smart City command centers) to auto-dispatch repair work orders.

Phase 6

In-Cabin Audio Safety Alerts

Real-time turn-by-turn voice alerts via Apple CarPlay and Android Auto: "Caution: severe crater reported 200m ahead in right lane."

Phase 7

Monsoon Waterlogging Forecaster

Correlating historical flood reports with open meteorological precipitation forecasts to alert commuters before underpasses become impassable.
