    How it works

    Three simple steps. Log in to submit your own report.

    ⚑

    Report a hazard

    Choose a type, write a short description, and click the map to set the location. You can attach an optional road photo.

    ▣

    See nearby risks

    Saved reports show up as map markers. Click a marker to view the hazard type, description, and status.

    ◎

    Track your reports

    Sign in to report hazards and find your submissions on the My Reports page. Administrators review and update statuses.

    About RoadSense

    RoadSense is a student project for sharing road safety problems. Phase 3 adds user accounts, report ownership, and an admin dashboard for reviewing reports.

    If you see a hazard on the road today, follow local safety guidance and contact the appropriate authorities as well.
