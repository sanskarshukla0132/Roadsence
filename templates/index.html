    03 — Mission & Technology

    About RoadSense

    RoadSense is an intelligent civic road safety platform that turns community hazard reports into verified geospatial intelligence. We bridge everyday commuters and municipal authorities to eliminate unflagged potholes, damaged roads, and waterlogging.
